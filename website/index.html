Orbit

A macOS menu bar app for personal SLURM monitoring.

Orbit visualizes and tracks the jobs and arrays owned by your account over SSH. It does not require a separate monitoring stack on the cluster.

How Orbit gets data

Scheduler output returns through the login node over SSH. Orbit parses it and stores observations in SQLite on your Mac.
